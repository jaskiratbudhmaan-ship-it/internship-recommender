THE PROBLEM

Finding an internship shouldn't feel like finding a needle in a haystack.

Students often have to search through hundreds of internship listings without knowing which opportunities actually fit their profile.

A student may have the right skills but still miss a relevant opportunity simply because it was difficult to discover.

InternMatch focuses on reducing that search gap by making recommendations more personalized.
