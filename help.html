Common questions

01

How do I create my profile?

Open the Register page and enter your basic details, education, skills and career preferences.

02

How are internships matched?

InternMatch compares information from your profile with internship requirements to generate relevant recommendations.

03

Where can I see my matches?

Open the My Matches section after signing in to view internships recommended for your profile.

04

Can I search internships?

Yes. Visit the Internships page to browse available opportunities and use the search options.

05

I entered the wrong information.

You can update your profile information once the profile-edit functionality is connected to the backend.

06

Where do I start?

Create your profile first. Then explore internships and check your personalised matches.
